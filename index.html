Eid ul Adha
Mubarak

Bakrid  ·  1446 H
عيد الأضحى مبارك

May the divine blessings of Allah fill your heart with joy,
bring peace to your home, and
accept the Qurbani and Hajj of all pilgrims.

May this blessed day of sacrifice remind us
of Ibrahim's (AS) devotion and obedience,
and may Allah's infinite mercy surround
you and your loved ones always.

❝ The best of days in this world is the Day of Nahr (Sacrifice). ❞
— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Abu Dawud)
